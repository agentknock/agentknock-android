BIP-39 English word list

The pairing address word list in res/raw/pairing_address_words.txt is the
unmodified BIP-39 English word list.

BIP-39 authors: Marek Palatinus, Pavol Rusnak, Aaron Voisine, and Sean Bowe.
Specification: https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0039.mediawiki
Word list: https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/ce1862ac6bcffa1dd20aad858380e51e66e949ea/bip-0039/english.txt
